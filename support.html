Ways to Support Contact Us
WHY SUPPORT SAVE

Strong communities begin with shared action.

Rural development becomes stronger when communities have access to knowledge, skills, livelihood opportunities, financial awareness and supportive community institutions. SAVE Foundation works alongside communities to help create these opportunities.

🌱

Support Agriculture

Support farmer skill building, improved agricultural practices, awareness of new technologies, seed and agricultural input opportunities and appropriate financial linkages for cultivation.

🐄

Support Livestock

Support rural families exploring livestock-based livelihoods through suitable financial linkages, fodder support and awareness of scientific livestock management practices.

👩‍🌾

Strengthen SHGs

Support Self-Help Groups through financial literacy, community participation, savings and credit awareness and appropriate connections with formal financial institutions.

💼

Support Livelihoods

Support rural families in building skills, exploring suitable livelihood opportunities, accessing relevant financial linkages and developing connections with potential markets.

HOW SUPPORT HELPS

From support to community opportunity.

Our approach focuses on strengthening people, skills, opportunities and community institutions.

01

Understand

02

03

04

Strengthen Communities

Encourage collective action and stronger community institutions for sustainable development.
