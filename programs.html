OUR PROGRAMMES

Building stronger communities, together.

SAVE Foundation works with rural communities through integrated programmes that strengthen livelihoods, empower people, protect natural resources and promote sustainable village development.

WHAT WE FOCUS ON

Creating opportunities where they matter most.

Our programmes are designed around the needs, strengths and aspirations of rural communities. Through community participation, skill development, livelihood support and institutional strengthening, SAVE Foundation works towards resilient and self-reliant villages.

01
🌱

Agriculture & Allied Livelihoods

Supporting farmers and rural families through sustainable agriculture, improved practices, skill development, livestock opportunities and appropriate financial linkages.

SAVE Foundation supports rural communities through practical agricultural knowledge, skill building and awareness of improved farming practices and new technologies.

  • 🌱 Farmer skill building and training
  • 💡 New agricultural technologies
  • 🌾 Seed and agricultural input support
  • 💰 Appropriate loan and financial linkages
  • 🐄 Livestock-based livelihood opportunities
  • 🤝 Market linkage opportunities

💧

Natural Resource Management

Promoting responsible use, conservation and community participation in the management of soil, water and other local natural resources.

SAVE Foundation promotes community awareness and participation in the responsible management of natural resources. The focus is on understanding local resources, encouraging sustainable practices and supporting communities in protecting the resources that are important for their livelihoods and future.

  • 💧 Water resource awareness and conservation
  • 🌱 Soil conservation and sustainable land practices
  • 🌿 Natural resource protection
  • 👥 Community participation in resource management
  • 💡 Awareness on sustainable resource use
  • 🌍 Community-focused environmental practices

Women Empowerment

Strengthening women's participation, financial awareness, skills, leadership and livelihood opportunities through Self-Help Groups and community-based initiatives.

SAVE Foundation works with women and Self-Help Groups to strengthen financial awareness, participation and access to suitable opportunities. Through community-based support, women can build knowledge, skills and confidence while exploring livelihood and financial opportunities.

  • 👩‍🌾 Self-Help Group promotion
  • 💡 Financial literacy and awareness
  • 💰 Savings and responsible borrowing awareness
  • 🏦 Appropriate bank loan linkages
  • 🛠️ Skill and livelihood development
  • 🤝 Women's participation in community institutions

Youth Development

SAVE Foundation encourages young people to participate in community development and explore opportunities that can strengthen their skills and future livelihoods. Through practical learning, awareness and community participation, youth can develop knowledge and confidence for their future.

  • 🎓 Skill development and practical learning
  • 💡 Career and livelihood awareness
  • 🛠️ Vocational and practical skills
  • 🚀 Entrepreneurship awareness
  • 🤝 Youth participation in community activities
  • 🌱 Opportunities for local livelihood development

Promoting practical skills, entrepreneurship awareness and suitable livelihood opportunities that can help individuals and families strengthen their economic participation.

SAVE Foundation supports individuals and communities in identifying suitable skills and exploring livelihood and entrepreneurship opportunities. Through practical training, knowledge sharing and guidance, people can strengthen their capabilities and explore opportunities based on their interests, resources and local needs.

  • 💡 Skill building and practical training
  • 🛠️ Development of livelihood-related skills

SAVE Foundation promotes education as an important part of sustainable community development. The programme focuses on creating awareness, encouraging learning opportunities and supporting communities in recognizing the importance of education for children, youth and families.

Health & Nutrition

SAVE Foundation promotes awareness of healthy practices and the importance of nutrition within rural communities. The programme encourages families and community groups to understand everyday health and nutrition practices that can contribute to healthier and more resilient communities.
