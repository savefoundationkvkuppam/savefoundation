ABOUT SAVE FOUNDATION

Growing stronger villages, together.

SAVE Foundation — Sustainable Agriculture and Village Enrichment — works with communities to encourage sustainable livelihoods, strengthen local participation and support inclusive village development.

Who We Are

SAVE Foundation stands for Sustainable Agriculture and Village Enrichment.

The foundation is built around the idea that stronger communities can grow when local people are actively involved in identifying challenges, developing practical solutions and creating sustainable opportunities.

Our approach brings together agriculture, livelihoods, community participation, women-led initiatives, livestock support and village development.

Our focus is people-centered development that respects local knowledge, community participation and long-term sustainability.
OUR STORY

More than development. It's about people.

SAVE Foundation was established with a vision to strengthen rural communities through sustainable agriculture, natural resource management and diversified livelihoods. By placing farmers and communities at the centre of development, we work to enhance agricultural resilience, improve household livelihoods, protect natural resources and create sustainable pathways for village development.

WHAT WE DO

Creating opportunities where they matter most.

SAVE Foundation works across key areas that contribute to stronger livelihoods, healthier communities and sustainable village development.

🌱

Agriculture

Supporting farmers through skill building, new technologies, seed support and financial linkages for cultivation.

SAVE Foundation supports farmers through skill building, practical training and awareness on improved agricultural practices and new technologies. We promote access to quality seeds and suitable agricultural inputs while helping farmers explore appropriate loan and financial linkages for cultivation needs. Through knowledge sharing and community-based support, farmers are encouraged to strengthen their agricultural livelihoods.

  • 🌱 Farmer skill building and training
  • 💡 Introduction to new agricultural technologies
  • 🌾 Seed and agricultural input support
  • 💰 Loan and financial linkage for cultivation
  • 👨‍🌾 Improved and sustainable farming practices
🐄

Livestock

Supporting rural families through livestock purchase loan linkages, improved fodder practices and scientific livestock management.

SAVE Foundation supports rural families interested in cattle, goats and other livestock-based activities by facilitating suitable financial and loan linkages for livestock purchase. We also promote improved fodder practices, including suitable fodder seeds, and encourage scientific livestock management through practical guidance and community-based support.

  • 🐄 Loan linkage for livestock purchase
  • 🐐 Support for cattle and goat-based livelihoods
  • 🌱 Introduction of suitable fodder seeds
  • 🥬 Improved fodder management
  • 💡 Scientific livestock management
  • 🤝 Practical guidance for livestock families
👩‍🌾

SHG Promotion

Strengthening Self-Help Groups through financial literacy, community participation and appropriate bank loan linkages.

SAVE Foundation works with Self-Help Groups (SHGs) to strengthen financial literacy and community participation. Through awareness and guidance, SHG members can learn about savings, responsible borrowing, loan processes and financial planning. We also support appropriate bank loan linkages and connections with formal financial institutions to explore opportunities for household and livelihood needs.

  • 💡 Financial literacy and awareness
  • 🏦 Bank loan linkage
  • 💰 Understanding savings and credit
  • 👩‍🌾 SHG member skill building
  • 🤝 Strengthening community groups
  • 📋 Responsible financial planning
💼

Livelihood Development

Supporting rural families through skill building, livelihood finance and market linkage opportunities based on their needs.

SAVE Foundation works with rural families and communities to understand their skills, interests, resources and livelihood needs. Through skill building and practical training, we support families in exploring suitable livelihood activities. Where appropriate, communities can be connected with relevant financial and loan linkages for their livelihood requirements. The foundation also promotes market linkage opportunities, helping communities explore suitable buyers, markets and channels for their products and services.

  • 💡 Skill building and practical training
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Understanding family livelihood needs
  • 💰 Financial and loan linkage for livelihood activities
  • 🛠️ Support for suitable livelihood opportunities
  • 🤝 Community-based livelihood development
  • 🌾 Market linkage and buyer connections
