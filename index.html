SAVE Foundation — Sustainable Agriculture and Village Enrichment — is a community-focused foundation working to strengthen rural livelihoods through sustainable agriculture, financial support, community institutions and inclusive village development. Established on 16 December 2025, SAVE Foundation places farmers, families and village communities at the centre of development, creating practical pathways towards self-reliance and a sustainable future.

Sustainable Agriculture & Village Enrichment

We believe lasting development begins when communities have the knowledge, resources and confidence to shape their own future.

Strengthening livelihoods.
Empowering communities.

Sustainable Agriculture and Village Enrichment Foundation (SAVE Foundation) is a community-focused organization committed to creating sustainable and resilient rural communities. We work with farmers, women, youth, and village communities to strengthen livelihoods, promote sustainable agriculture, conserve natural resources, and improve the overall quality of rural life. Our approach is rooted in community participation, local knowledge, appropriate technology, and sustainable solutions that create lasting social, economic, and environmental impact

  • Promote sustainable farming practices
  • Strengthen rural livelihoods and skills
  • Support inclusive village development
  • Encourage community participation

OUR IMPACT

Creating Change at the Grassroots

We strive to create measurable and sustainable improvements in rural communities by focusing on practical opportunities, stronger institutions, resilient livelihoods and inclusive village development.

01

Increased Agricultural Productivity

Supporting improved agricultural practices and opportunities that can contribute to stronger farm livelihoods and incomes.

02

Improved Soil & Water Management

Encouraging responsible soil and water management practices that contribute to sustainable rural development.

03

Diversified & Resilient Livelihoods

Supporting families in exploring diversified livelihood opportunities that can strengthen household resilience.

04

Stronger Farmer & Community Institutions

Strengthening farmer groups, Self-Help Groups and community institutions through participation, skills and collective action.

05

Opportunities for Women & Youth

Promoting opportunities for women and youth through skills, participation, livelihood development and community-based initiatives.

06

Access to Knowledge, Technology & Markets

Helping communities explore access to relevant knowledge, technologies, financial opportunities and market linkages.

07

Enhanced Environmental Sustainability

Encouraging environmentally responsible practices and community participation in natural resource conservation.

08

Better Quality of Life in Rural Villages

Contributing to stronger livelihoods, community participation and improved opportunities for families and villages.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Goal

Working together today to create stronger and more sustainable villages tomorrow.

🎯

Our Mission

"To empower rural communities through sustainable agriculture, livelihood development, natural resource management, capacity building, and community-led initiatives that promote lasting social, economic, and environmental well-being."

👁️

Our Vision

"To build prosperous, inclusive, and environmentally sustainable villages where farming communities thrive with dignity, resilience, and equal opportunities."

🚩

Our Goal

“To create resilient and self-reliant villages by strengthening sustainable farming, protecting natural resources, enhancing livelihoods, empowering communities and promoting inclusive development.”
